# Contributing

Use the prompt suggestion issue template to propose an addition.

Please include:

- the original creator and source URL;
- the complete prompt, or a direct link to where the creator published it;
- the Qwen Image version used;
- required reference images or other inputs;
- an output preview when the creator has made one publicly available;
- any important limitation, disclosure or cross-tool step.

Submissions are reviewed for attribution, duplication and practical reuse before inclusion. Opening an issue does not guarantee publication.

Do not submit private material, personal data, paywalled content, or work you are not allowed to share. Creators may request corrections or removal through an issue.
